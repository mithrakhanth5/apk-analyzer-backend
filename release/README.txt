APK RISK ANALYZER - RELEASE PACKAGE
=====================================

Contents:
1. app-release.apk  (The Mobile App)
2. run_backend.bat  (The Server Starter)
3. backend/         (Server Files)

INSTRUCTIONS
============

STEP 1: START THE BACKEND (PC)
------------------------------
The mobile app relies on your PC to perform the heavy analysis.
1. Keep your PC turned on.
2. Double-click `run_backend.bat` in this folder.
3. Wait until you see "Application startup complete".
4. DO NOT close that black window.

STEP 2: INSTALL THE APP (PHONE)
-------------------------------
1. Transfer `app-release.apk` to your Android phone (via USB, Drive, or Email).
2. Install the APK.
3. Ensure your phone is connected to the SAME WI-FI as this PC.

TROUBLESHOOTING
===============
- "Connection Failed": 
  - Ensure PC and Phone are on the same Wi-Fi.
  - Disable PC Firewall temporarily if issues persist.
  - The App is configured to connect to: 10.62.214.100:8000

- "App Crashes":
  - Ensure the backend window is running and shows no errors.

ENJOY!
